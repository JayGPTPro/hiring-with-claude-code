# hire-first-month

From the offer call to day thirty for one remote hire. Before the call: the offer letter and a contract checklist, worded for the structure you chose (contractor, employer of record or your own local company), with a not-legal-advice line on the letter. After the yes: your role scorecard becomes the onboarding plan (five tasks mapped by day 14, one AI workflow with human checks by day 60, the SOP and handoff by day 90), an access plan with least privilege (a personal account, Seller Central view-only until you approve more, no customer data in the first month, a password manager, two-factor), check-ins on day 7, 14 and 30 on working days, a day-30 table of Expected result / Actual evidence / Missing training / Next action, and the offboarding list. The assistant writes files; you send, sign, pay, grant and import the calendar file.

Install: `npx skills add JayGPTPro/hiring-with-claude-code`

Before the offer call, from your `hiring/` folder or the folder above it:
`/hire-first-month I'm about to make the offer to [name]`

After they accept:
`/hire-first-month [name] accepted. Build the 30-day plan, the access plan and the check-ins`
