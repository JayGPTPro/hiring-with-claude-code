## Day 30: {cp30_date}, the check-in

One hour, both of you. Three questions: did the work happen, what still needs training, which expectations were unrealistic? The same scorecard that chose the person shows whether the role is working, for both of you. Fill the evidence column before the call (or use `day-30.html`).

{day30_table}

### Before any conclusion: three checks

1. **Were the expectations realistic?** Ramp to full speed takes about three months: weeks 1 and 2 orientation, weeks 3 and 4 steady quality, month 2 wider duties, month 3 full speed. A month-3 expectation is not a day-30 miss.
2. **Was the training given?** For every gap: was it documented, taught or asked for in writing? If not, the gap is in the onboarding; write the training into the third column.
3. **Were the conditions there?** Access granted on time, the data available, your replies inside the overlap window, the time not taken by other work. Ask {first_name}: what got in the way?

### What follows

- **On track:** say it on the call. Grant the Month 3 levels (`access-plan.md`). The raise schedule stays as written in the offer ({raise_text_short}).
- **A gap with a cause you can fix** (training, access, data, an unclear expectation): fix it this week, write the next action with a date, and check it at day 60.
- **A gap after the expectations were clear, the training was given and the conditions were there:** write it down with the evidence, tell {first_name} in plain words the same day, and agree what day 60 must show. Any decision about the role is yours, made at the review the agreement sets, with the evidence in front of you.

This check-in never ends with an automatic "end it".
