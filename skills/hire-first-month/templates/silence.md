# When replies get shorter: {name}

Written to `hiring/out/first-month/silence.md`. The assistant drafts; you send, and only when the moment comes.

Silence is usually shame or a skill gap, not disrespect. Someone who is stuck and afraid to say so goes quiet; someone who is bored or has another client goes slow. Neither one is fixed by a sharper tone. This is agency practice and founder stories, not a study, but it changes what you do, so it is written here.

## The ladder

1. **Said in week one, in words:** asking for help is never punished. It is in the onboarding doc and in the first security talk. Say it again at the day 7 call.
2. **The day the replies get shorter or slower**, check in that day. Not a message about the work; a call, five minutes: "You seem quieter this week. What is in the way?"
3. **If it does not change, or the person goes silent,** send one calm message that restates the expectations and gives a reply-by date. The message is below. One message, not three.
4. **If nothing comes back by the date:** revoke access the same day (`offboarding.md`), close the contract under its notice clause, and write down the open work so the next person can pick it up. Pay what is owed to the day; a withheld payment is the one thing that turns a quiet exit into a bad one.

## The one calm message

Hi {first_name},

I have not heard from you since [date of the last message]. I want to check that you are okay first; if something happened, tell me and we will work around it.

On the work: the daily report at the end of each day and the overlap hours ({overlap_window_hire} your time) are the two things I need to run this. [One sentence on the open piece of work and what state it is in.]

Please reply by [day, date], even if it is only to tell me you need more time. If I do not hear from you by then I will take it that you have stepped away, close the open work on my side and pay you for the days worked.

{signoff}

## Notes for you, not for the message

- One sentence about the open work, no list of everything that slipped.
- The reply-by date is two working days out, not tomorrow.
- Send it in the channel you normally use with them, in the overlap window, so it does not land at night.
