CONTROL CV TEMPLATE. This file is a shape, not a control: redact.py never scores it.
When hiring/control-cv.txt does not exist, Claude writes it from this shape and criteria.md before the
first redact (SKILL.md, step 2). You can also write it yourself.

The control is one fictional application with a known band. It rides in every batch as
out/blind/C000.txt and is scored like the others. When it lands outside its band, the rubric moved that
batch.

- A middle candidate, not a star and not a no: two claims with a number and a date, one big claim with no
  detail behind it, one must-have that is not shown, an ask inside the target.
- The areas and must-haves from criteria.md, the same kind of employer the applicants come from. Every
  company invented; no real person.
- On the second line, the band it was built to land in. Never change the text between batches.

Everything from the next line down is the file redact.py copies to out/blind/C000.txt.
