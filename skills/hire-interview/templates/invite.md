# Call invite (the message that books the interview)

Saved as `hiring/out/messages/<slug>-interview-invite.md`. The assistant never sends it; you do, in the
channel you have used with that person. Five things live in this message: the recording notice and the
notes-only option (asked here and again on the call), the AI assistant line, the date the raw recording is
deleted, the screen share line, and the reference sentence. Keep all five.

---

Hi {first_name},

Thanks for your application. I would like to talk: a 30 minute video call, {day} at {time} your time. Link: {call_link}. If that does not work, send me two other times.

So there are no surprises: I record and transcribe my interviews so I can listen instead of typing. An AI assistant helps me organise the transcript and my notes; the decision is mine. The recording is deleted on {deletion_date} and only my written notes stay. If you would rather I take notes only, say so and that is what I will do. I will ask again at the start of the call.

I will ask you to share your screen and show one thing you built or run for real work. If you are on a Mac, please allow your browser under System Settings, Privacy & Security, Screen Recording, and try sharing once before the call.

If you reach the last step, I would ask you for one or two people who managed or worked with you. I only call people you approve, and never your current employer.

{signoff}

---

Fill-in rules:

- `{deletion_date}`: a real date, the same one as in criteria.md. Raw recordings and transcripts go on that
  date; the debrief, the scores and the decision stay.
- The reference sentence goes to everyone or to no one. It is routine, not a threat.
- Nothing about family, children, health or where they are from. The time zone line is about the clock.
- Ask what consent your own law requires for recording. A candidate who says no to recording gets the same
  interview, the same questions and the same scoring sheet; their debrief says "notes only".
