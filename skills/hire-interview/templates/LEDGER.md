## The method's rules

`ledger.py` adds this list to the end of LEDGER.md. Add a rule only when a call teaches something that is not here.

1. Recording and transcript on before the call, with the candidate's knowledge. Notes only is always an option.
2. Mac users: the invite asks them to allow screen recording and to try sharing once before the call.
3. Two minutes of warm-up, not scored. A first impression is not a score.
4. The same five core questions for everyone, plus one tailored follow-up from their evidence map. Anchors for a 1, a 3 and a 5 are written before the call.
5. Your score and one line of evidence first. Claude's analysis is opened only after your sheet is saved.
6. A question you did not ask is unknown, not the candidate's weakness. It is left out of the total.
7. Follow the number: when someone gives a result, ask what changed and who owned it.
8. Before the close, look at the six boxes and ask any question that is still empty.
9. Ask again and wait. No hints that carry the answer.
10. Word share is a coaching indicator, not a target and not a candidate score.
11. A short transcript line can carry the wrong speaker. A disputed quote is unverified until someone checks the recording; it is corrected only with an audit note, otherwise it stays unresolved and unused.
12. The coach observes behaviour only. It never reads personality, motivation or honesty from a pause, a smile or a quiet answer.
13. Work questions only. Never family, children, relationship status, health, age, religion or plans outside work.
14. When it is clearly not a match by minute ten, ask the two most important remaining questions, give them room for their questions, and close kindly.
