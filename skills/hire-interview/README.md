# hire-interview

The interview stage, in three steps. First a claim check: only the job claims you name, checked against the candidate's own documents (CV, message, questionnaire) and public work sources, never a search on their name and never their personal life. Each claim gets Verified, Unverified or Clarification needed and one neutral question, which lands on the prep page. Then a prep page per candidate: Evidence / Missing / Questions, the same five core questions with 1 / 3 / 5 anchors written before the call, one tailored follow-up and a scoring sheet. After each call, one script turns the Google Meet (.docx or .txt) or Zoom (.vtt) transcript into the format the others read, marking estimated minutes with a ~. Your own score is saved before Claude's analysis exists. Then two files, CANDIDATE_DEBRIEF.md (evidence with quotes, open questions, disputed quotes unverified until checked against the recording) and INTERVIEWER_COACH.md (word share, questions covered even when you asked them in your own words, missed follow-ups, one thing to improve), and a ledger with two tabs that shows averages when candidates were asked different numbers of questions. The assistant never contacts anyone and never decides.

Install: `npx skills add JayGPTPro/hiring-with-claude-code`

First command, for each person you will interview: `Use the hire-interview skill's claim check for <name>. Check only these job claims: <claim 1>, <claim 2>.` Then `/hire-interview prep`. After a call, give Claude the transcript file and your score sheet and run `/hire-interview <name>`.
