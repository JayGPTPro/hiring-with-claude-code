# hire-test-task

The last step of hiring. Terms in writing first, the same for every finalist. Then a goal-first brief with one shared task built from your list of things waiting (real, or made up), plus an optional part in each finalist's strongest area, about two hours. You can pay or not: a short task on made-up data can be unpaid, and then the work is never used; a longer task or one on your real material gets a fee recommendation, and you decide. A scoring guide is written with the brief and locked before the work comes in. You rate each submission first; Claude's notes against the guide come after, with quotes. A walkthrough of one decision per finalist. Then the Decision Challenge: your choice is sealed before Claude says anything, and Claude answers with For, Against, Missing evidence and Next check. Last, the offer, runner-up and closing drafts, which wait until your hire has signed. The assistant writes files; you send, pay and decide.

Install: `npx skills add JayGPTPro/hiring-with-claude-code`

First command, from your `hiring/` folder or the folder above it:
`/hire-test-task write the terms, the briefs and the scoring guide for my finalists`
