# Closing (everyone else who is still waiting on you)

Saved as `hiring/out/messages/<slug>-closing.md`. The assistant never sends it, and it waits
until the hire has signed.
Who gets one: anyone you interviewed, anyone you promised to get back to, anyone who
filled the questionnaire and asked. The exact sentence is fixed; use it as written.

---

Hi {first_name},

Thank you for applying and for the time you put in.

The role has been filled. If I need another person I will go back through the applications.

{signoff}

---

Variants, same two fixed sentences in the middle:

- After an interview: add before the closing line, "I enjoyed our conversation, and {one specific thing}."
- A candidate who withdrew: "Thanks for letting me know. The role has been filled. If I need another person I will go back through the applications."
- A candidate who asked and never got a reply: open with the late-reply apology (see late-reply-apology.md).
