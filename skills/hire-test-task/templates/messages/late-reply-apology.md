# Late reply apology (one line, at the top of any message that is more than two days late)

Put it on the first line, then continue with the message as it is. One line, no reason,
no story about the busy week.

---

Sorry for the slow reply.

---

Then the message. Two examples:

Sorry for the slow reply.
Thank you for applying and for the time you put in.
The role has been filled. If I need another person I will go back through the applications.
{signoff}

Sorry for the slow reply.
Thank you for the test task; the way you checked the numbers before the summary was exactly right.
The role has been filled. If I need another person I will go back through the applications.
{signoff}
