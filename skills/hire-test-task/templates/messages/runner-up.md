# Runner-up (a finalist who did the test task and is not getting this role)

Saved as `hiring/out/messages/<slug>-runner-up.md`. The assistant never sends it, and it waits
until the hire has signed: "the role has been filled" is only true then.
The candidate hears that the role has been filled, one specific thing you valued in their work,
and, for a paid task, that the fee is paid. When the task was unpaid, leave the fee line out.
Do not say "narrowed it down" and do not rank them.

A second role: mention it only if it is real, and say plainly that it is not a promise. One
sentence, after the fixed lines, for example: "I may need a second person for reporting next
year. That is not a promise, but if it happens I would like to ask you first."

---

Hi {first_name},

Thank you for the {task, in two words}. {One specific sentence about what was good in it, in your words.}

The role has been filled. If I need another person I will go back through the applications.

The {fee} was sent to you {day}. If it has not arrived by {day + 3}, tell me.

{signoff}
