# Scoring guide: the test task

For you only, never sent. Written with the briefs, locked before the first submission arrives
(`build_briefs.py --lock-guide`), and used for your ratings and for Claude's notes. After the lock
it does not change; if you want to change it, say so in writing and rate everyone again.

## The four questions

The same for every finalist and for both parts. Rate 1 to 5, one line of evidence each.

| Question | 5 looks like | 2 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Did they get the goal? | Built for the goal in the brief; made a choice the ask did not spell out | Did the ask literally, or solved a different problem |
| Is it about my business? | Uses this business's numbers, dates, people and limits | Could be sent to any business, or needs systems the business does not have |
| Can I see why they chose it? | Says why, what was left out, what is missing, and where a person checks | Conclusions without reasons; "a human in the loop" with no place named |
| Would I use it on Monday? | You could act on it on Monday morning, maybe after one small fix | You would rebuild it before using it |

Part 1 (the shared task) is the fair comparison. Part 2 (the specialist part) is rated against
its own track; a listings part and a reporting part are not compared with each other.
