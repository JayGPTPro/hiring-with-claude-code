# Test task agreement: {name}

This is a checklist in one paragraph, not legal advice. It says who may use the work, and it
keeps the task honest on both sides. If you want a contract, show this to a lawyer in your
country and theirs; the five points are the ones to keep. The fee lines come from `fee_usd` in
config.json, so it reads right for a paid and an unpaid task. `build_briefs.py` writes a copy for
each finalist next to the brief, as `challenge/briefs/<slug>-trial-agreement.md`.

---

**Between {brand} ({signoff}) and {name}, for the test task "{title}".**

The task takes about {effort} and is due {due}. {fee_terms} {use_terms} {name} does the task personally and does not pass any part of it to someone else. {name} works on copies and sample data only, not on {brand}'s live systems, accounts or customer data, and nothing from the task is to be sent to customers. {stop_terms}

Agreed on {date}.

{name}: ______________________

{signoff}, for {brand}: ______________________

---

The five points, so you can check any other version against them:

1. The effort, the deadline, and the fee or "unpaid", in writing.
2. Paid: you may use this work, and nothing else they make. Unpaid: you use none of it.
3. Nothing from the task is published or shared without asking them.
4. No subcontracting of the task.
5. Trialists stay off live systems and customer data.
